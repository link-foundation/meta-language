## Actual code and independent controls

`rust-source-field-images.js` separates each actual component's containing
lifetime from its whole-read revision. Both remain local interpreter coordinates,
derived from actual allocation/declaration inventories. Whole image reads and
return ancestry include lifetime coordinates. Nested direct projections retain
actual ancestor lifetimes and selected components, without carrying an unrelated
intermediate parent's whole revision. Borrowed parents preserve their actual
original whole loans; readonly views grant no narrower ownership authority.

`rust-source-field-revisions.js` rebuilds only checked selected paths and their
declaration ancestors. Changed ancestors get new whole-read revisions with their
existing containing lifetimes. Untouched siblings retain their actual IDs,
availability and loans. A selected replacement gets a fresh complete subtree;
its old selected revision and containing lifetime end. Replacing a parent thus
invalidates loans into old descendants even where their local IDs still exist.
Multiple selected paths remain distinct/disjoint and bounded. Current images
are derived from actual fields, never a supplied mask or a disjointness flag.

Branch image joins reuse actual common components and lifetimes. Only differing
coordinates get joined revisions. Existing per-branch availability merges and
returned-region rebasing then retain those exact identities. Untouched references
outside the branch stay valid without relabeling them; overlapping stale aliases
remain unavailable. Resource bounds apply to traversals and allocated roots.

`rust-source-partial-state.js` shares that revision publisher across checked
assignments, closed helpers and standard String/Vec operations. Real storage
proof publication eagerly establishes its actual inventory/lifetime roots before
branch snapshots. Source facets, public datatype shapes and caller ABI keys do
not change. The new module is registered for later automatic native publication;
no Rust semantics were handwritten.

`rust-borrowed-source-regions.js` ends containing lifetimes on actual owning
transfer, whole overwrite, closed whole-root acquisition, owner scope exit,
pattern/iteration binder expiry and temporary expiry. Copying or transporting
state does not end a containing lifetime. Fresh scalar/String materialization
and owning transfer clear transient ancestor coordinates. Whole-root helper
outputs publish fresh actual storage proofs. `rust-source-state-transform-regions.js`
retains conservative whole-state acquisition, ensuring a finite helper cannot
silently preserve old descendant loans. `rust-source-loop-regions.js` carries
complete per-branch/step snapshots into joins and charges additional cells;
its existing complete-loop-state contract is retained.

Eighteen positive and twelve invalid originals are appended under the unchanged

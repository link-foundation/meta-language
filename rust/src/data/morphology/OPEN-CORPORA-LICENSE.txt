OpenCorpora contributors, Russian morphological dictionary and corpus.

The Russian vocabulary in russian.lino is a selection of the OpenCorpora
dictionary, source revision 417150, and tag probabilities from corpus
revision 4580142. The compiled snapshot is pymorphy3-dicts-ru
2.4.417150.4580142. The Links vocabulary selects complete inflection paradigms
and keeps the tags and probabilities of attested readings of each selected
spelling. The derived vocabulary and the optional upstream data are licensed
under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported.
